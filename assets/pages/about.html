About Me

I am a hard working mother who is just trying to balance everything. On top of academics and motherhood, I still work to support my family. We live in a society that requires both parents to work in order to live comfortably. Last year we were weeks from homelessness when I found this apartment and decided to go back to school. I am working so hard to not only get a degree but to show my daughter a better way of life. I want to teach her the importance of education and hard work.

My Goals

My temporary goal is to simply graduate and find a job in the field so I can provide a better lifefor my family. My long-term goal is to build a better future for my daughter. I want to own my own home and land. I have a dream to freely paint canvases with my daughter, in the yard I own.I want to continue to grow our fruits and vegetables. I want my daughter to have a same place to fall back on if she needs it. As far back as I can remember, I have desired stablity and the power over something, someplace no one can take away from me.
